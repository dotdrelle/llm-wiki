Design one new deliverable template for the requested family and intent, grounded in the current wiki structure, existing templates, workspace profile and applicable build context. Present an evidence-backed template plan for approval before writing, never invent unsupported sections, validate all referenced context, and finish by reporting the written paths and any unanchored section. Do not build the resulting deliverable. If a messaging connector and a notification recipient from the workspace profile are available, send a short best-effort terminal summary in the reply language; otherwise skip notification silently, and never let notification failure change the template-authoring outcome.
